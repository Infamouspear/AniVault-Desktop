# AniVault Desktop 1.4.0 dashboard

Home and Statistics count one local normal-scope anime entry or manga series per
library record. Franchise containers and remote-only titles do not add to totals.
Movies and specials are subsets of anime, based on known format/category.
Completed titles use assigned status; watched episodes count present indexed
episode files marked watched. Genres overlap and use all local titles as the
denominator. Storage sizes and historical activity are unavailable: reliable byte
totals and a separate timestamped playback event log are not currently indexed.

Manual check: open Home and compare its totals with Statistics. Resume an
episode or reading item, change a title status, and revisit both pages. Select a
recent addition and verify In Focus; double-click it to open details. Try the
quick actions and resize with compact navigation and the rail open and closed.
The published 1.3.1 release remains available for rollback.
